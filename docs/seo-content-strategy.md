# Shuvia organic content plan

The public site should earn search visibility with useful, specific guidance for couples, not a high volume of thin keyword pages. Keep relationship advice warm, inclusive, practical, and clearly distinct from product promotion. Have sensitive relationship guidance reviewed by a qualified editor before publication.

## Editorial backlog

| Working topic | Search intent | Useful reader outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-distance date ideas that work across time zones | Practical planning | A varied set of low-cost date formats with timing and accessibility tips |
| How to build a small daily ritual as a couple | Relationship habits | A realistic 10-minute routine and advice for restarting without guilt |
| Questions to ask on a virtual date | Conversation help | Original prompts grouped by light, playful, and reflective moods |
| How to make a thoughtful digital love note | Gift inspiration | Examples of personal details to include, without formulaic scripts |
| Celebrating an anniversary when you are apart | Occasion planning | A flexible plan that works across distance, budgets, and schedules |
| Staying connected when schedules do not line up | Communication guidance | Asynchronous rituals and clear expectations that do not demand constant availability |

## Publishing standard

- Publish only when an editor can add original examples, practical steps, and a clear author or reviewer.
- Cite reputable primary sources for factual or wellbeing claims; avoid presenting general content as therapy.
- Add a publication date, meaningful update date, descriptive title and summary, and links to genuinely relevant Shuvia features.
- Use original illustrations or properly licensed imagery with descriptive alternative text.
- Review traffic and usefulness before expanding a topic; merge or remove pages that become redundant.
- Add official social profile links and structured-data `sameAs` properties only after Shuvia controls and verifies those profiles.
